## Brand & Style

This design system targets high-performance runners, sneaker collectors, and technical sportswear enthusiasts who demand precision engineering and uncompromising visual clarity. The aesthetic balances high-contrast minimalism with athletic brutalism: pure white expanses disrupted by surgical black grid lines and energetic bursts of race red.

The interface evokes velocity, physical discipline, and technical mastery. Interactions feel instant, rigid, and deliberate—eschewing soft, organic flourishes in favor of razor-sharp edges, high-contrast structural framing, and bold typographic hierarchy inspired by race bibs, wind tunnel telemetry, and archival track spikes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an aggressive visual tempo through high contrast:
- **Primary (`#E5001A` / Race Red):** Reserved strictly for active indicators, checkout CTA actions, dropped-time records, stock scarcity warnings, and high-impact badges.
- **Secondary (`#0D0D0E` / Stark Carbon):** Grounds primary titles, structural 1px borders, key micro-labels, and high-emphasis action states.
- **Tertiary (`#767676` / Spec Grey):** Utility tone for sizing charts, technical telemetry, metadata, and breadcrumbs.
- **Neutral (`#F4F4F5` / Cool Track):** Surfaces product pedestal backdrops, inactive card containers, and secondary interactive layers over pure `#FFFFFF` base canvases.

Use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to spotlight silhouette photography, isolating product geometry against stark outlines.

## Typography

Typographic scale anchors the performance feel:
- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **Space Grotesk** to inject mechanical, aerodynamic character. Always set primary headlines in tight negative tracking with structural uppercase usage for collection headings and technical footwear model callouts.
- **Body & Product Descriptions:** Handled by **Work Sans** for immediate readability at velocity. Delivers neutral, highly legible copy across product specifications and editorial reviews.
- **Labels & Data Badges:** Rendered in **Space Grotesk** all-caps with positive tracking to simulate stamped sprint split markers, serial codes, and technical textile labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a strict 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, condensing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. Structural 1px border dividers align flush to grid lines, creating a paneled, technical layout reminiscent of spec sheets and blueprint framing.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** `margin-desktop` (3rem) canvas padding; 12 columns with `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem) intervals. Product grids feature alternating aspect ratios to emphasize silhouette motion.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** `margin-tablet` (2rem); 8 columns with 1rem gutters. Sticky side panels collapse into structured horizontal drawers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** `margin` (1rem); 4 columns with edge-to-edge full-bleed technical imagery framed by crisp top and bottom hairline borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, atmospheric drop shadows. Visual depth is established through physical architecture and contrasting boundary planes:

- **Structural Outlines:** Every surface, modal, and module is framed by crisp, 1px solid borders (`#0D0D0E` for high prominence; `#E4E4E7` for base containment).
- **Z-Index Offsets (Hard Shadows):** Where layering is required (e.g., sticky action toolbars or hovered buy-cards), use zero-blur directional offset drops: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #0D0D0E`.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Overlays and flyout filters utilize 85% opacity `#FFFFFF` with minimal backdrop blur, layered directly above contrasting light-grey surfaces (`#F4F4F5`) to maintain optical separation without losing edge crispness.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a completely sharp geometric silhouette (`roundedness: 0`). 

No rounded corners, pills, or softened chamfers appear within the core UI elements. Interactive buttons, product tiles, badges, size selectors, and modal windows feature exact 90-degree corners. This uncompromising architectural geometry underscores the engineered, aerodynamic precision of elite athletic gear.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy Now):* Solid `#0D0D0E` background, pure `#FFFFFF` uppercase `label-lg` text, 0px border-radius. On hover, background flashes to `#E5001A` with a hard drop shadow (`2px 2px 0px #0D0D0E`).
  - *Secondary / Outline:* Pure `#FFFFFF` background with a solid 1px `#0D0D0E` border and uppercase text. Active state triggers inverse black fill with white text.
  - *Destructive / Alert:* Solid `#E5001A` with `#FFFFFF` text.

- **Badges & Chips:**
  - *Spec Badges ("NEW DROP", "CARBON PLATE", "RACE DAY"):* Ultra-compact rectangular chips with 1px border. Dark variants use `#0D0D0E` background with white text; speed-tier variants use `#E5001A` background with white text.
  - *Filter Chips:* Monospaced appearance using `label-md`. Inactive state sits on `#FFFFFF` with `#E4E4E7` border; selected state fills `#0D0D0E` with white text.

- **Product Cards:**
  - Enclosed in a 1px `#0D0D0E` or `#E4E4E7` frame over an off-white (`#F4F4F5`) photo container.
  - Footwear images sit against clean white or pale neutral backdrops with zero drop shadow under the shoe, floating technical product specs directly above the bottom border.
  - Hover triggers an instant swap to technical isometric/top-down outsole views and elevates the card via a `4px 4px 0px #0D0D0E` offset.

- **Input Fields & Size Selectors:**
  - *Text Inputs:* 1px `#0D0D0E` outline, squared corners, 16px horizontal padding. Labels sit flush outside or pinned to the upper-left boundary in `label-sm` uppercase.
  - *Shoe Size Matrix:* Rigid grid of sharp square tiles. Out-of-stock sizes feature a diagonal 1px strike-through line; selected sizes take an immediate `#0D0D0E` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Pure geometric squares. Unchecked is a 1.5px `#0D0D0E` border; checked contains an inner solid `#E5001A` square inset by 2px, avoiding checkmarks or rounded dots.

- **Domain-Specific Components:**
  - *Pace & Mileage Telemetry Strip:* Horizontal data ticker displaying shoe drop (mm), weight (oz), and stack height, styled like digital sprint split monitors.
  - *Drop Countdown Module:* Monospace-style tabular block displaying hours/minutes/seconds until product launch, framed inside high-contrast black and crimson paneling.
